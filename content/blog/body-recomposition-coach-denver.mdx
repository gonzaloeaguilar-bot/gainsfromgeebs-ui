## What a recomposition coach looks at

A body recomposition coach tracks more than weight. The useful inputs are photos, waist trend, strength performance, nutrition consistency, recovery, and whether the plan survives normal work weeks.

For many clients, the first win is not a new exercise. It is having someone notice when the plan is drifting and make a clear adjustment.

That is why the online model works well for busy Denver professionals: the coaching is present between sessions.

## Who this fits
