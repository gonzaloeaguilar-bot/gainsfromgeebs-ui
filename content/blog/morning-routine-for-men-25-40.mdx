## Keep the routine simple

A useful morning routine for a working man should make the rest of the day easier, not create another long checklist. Start with a few repeatable anchors: get some light, drink water, eat protein, and move before the workday takes over.

## Build around your real morning

The order matters less than whether the routine survives a normal week. Pick a version you can repeat on busy days, then make anything beyond that optional. A short walk and a planned first meal are more useful than an elaborate routine you abandon after a few mornings.

## Let the routine support training

Your morning does not need to include a full workout to help your fitness. It can protect the time you plan to train later, make breakfast easier to manage, and give the day a clear start. If morning training fits your schedule, prepare the night before so fewer decisions stand between waking up and starting.
